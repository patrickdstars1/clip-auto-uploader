Clip Auto Uploader

Clip Auto Uploader is a small personal tool that uploads short videos I have created to YouTube channels that I own and operate. It uses the YouTube Data API videos.insert method and requests only the youtube.upload scope.

What it does

  • I sign in to my own Google account through the standard Google OAuth 2.0 consent screen.
  • I choose a video file from my own computer and enter its title, description and tags.
  • The tool uploads that video to my own YouTube channel.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect, store or share data about other users, videos, comments or channels.
  • It does not display or embed YouTube content on any website.
  • It does not sell advertising and generates no revenue.
